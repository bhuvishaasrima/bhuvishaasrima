<h2>About Me</h2>

I'm Bhuvishaa, an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at Thiagarajar College of Engineering.

My academic and technical interests lie at the intersection of theoretical concepts and practical hardware implementation. I enjoy exploring how computational concepts translate into efficient hardware implementations, combining mathematical modeling, digital logic, and practical circuit design.

I am particularly drawn to the design of electronic systems that bring together hardware architecture, signal processing, and real-world functionality.
