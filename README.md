  &nbsp; ABOUT ME
</h2>

I'm Bhuvishaa, an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at Thiagarajar College of Engineering.

My work and academic interests lie at the intersection of theoretical concepts and practical hardware implementation. I enjoy exploring how computational ideas translate into efficient electronic systems, from digital logic and programmable hardware to signal processing and physical circuit design.

I am particularly drawn to the design of systems that combine hardware architecture, mathematical modeling, and real-world functionality.
